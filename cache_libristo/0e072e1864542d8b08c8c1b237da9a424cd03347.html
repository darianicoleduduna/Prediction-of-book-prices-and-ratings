However, I need to get something off my chest before I start praising this book.

ELSA. GIRL. PLEASE. FOR THE LOVE OF EVERYTHING THAT IS HOLY, STOP. 🤦‍♀️😭

At the beginning of this book, Elsa was getting on my nerves SO FUCKING MUCH. I wanted to grab her by the shoulders and tell her to PLEASE, JUST STFU FOR FIVE MINUTES. I was genuinely struggling with her, and there were moments when I had to remind myself that characters are allowed to be frustrating and that I don't have to agree with every single thing they do.

BUT THEN. THEN RINA KENT DECIDED TO GIVE US BOTH ELSA'S AND AIDEN'S POVs. FINALLY. FINALLY, MY PRAYERS HAVE BEEN ANSWERED!

And this is something I genuinely appreciate in dark romance. I believe that having both the FMC's and MMC's perspectives can make an enormous difference, especially when the male lead is morally questionable and the relationship itself is complicated. Seeing what BOTH characters are thinking and feeling doesn't magically excuse their behavior, but it gives us a better understanding of their motivations, their emotional baggage, and the things they struggle to express.

I don't want to spend an entire book hating a character because I can only see one side of the story, only to discover later that there was so much more going on beneath the surface.
