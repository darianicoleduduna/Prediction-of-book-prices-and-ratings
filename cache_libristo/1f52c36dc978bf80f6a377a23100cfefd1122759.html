Blood & Betrayals

Jeanette Rose, Alexis Rune

Fear, Fury and Fire.

To Know is Not Enough.

Power flows through the ancient halls of Avalon University like a neverending flood. Admission to the elite institution enables students to hone their magic under the watchful eye of their icy headmaster.

Summer Tuatha De Daanan planned to slide through her tenure at Avalon, unnoticed and inconspicuous. She has no interest in learning more about her past or why someone applied to the school on her behalf. Her emotional walls keep everyone at bay, and her sharp tongue makes people give her a wide berth. Yet, despite her best attempts, her emotional armor begins to melt from her feral vampire roommate to the angelic senior who is never dissuaded by her feigning disinterest. Maybe this could be good for her, a new life. Yet, dark secrets lurk in the halls of Avalon, and soon those same halls are drenched in blood.

Summer finds herself as the obsession of the sadistic stalker with the Seven lessons to learn. With the help of her new friends, she races against the clock to solve the mystery, hoping to save lives and catch the killer before it's too late.

“ꜰᴏʀ ᴀʟʟ ᴛʜᴇ ʀᴇᴀᴅᴇʀꜱ ᴡʜᴏ ᴡᴀᴛᴄʜᴇᴅ ᴛʜᴇɪʀ ᴡᴏʀʟᴅ ʙᴜʀɴ ᴛᴏ ᴀꜱʜ, ᴀɴᴅ ᴄᴏᴜʟᴅ ᴅᴏ ɴᴏᴛʜɪɴɢ ʙᴜᴛ ꜱᴄʀᴇᴀᴍ.”

Some dedications feel like a whisper.
This one felt like a warning.

And I should’ve known right then and there to brace myself.

Because this book doesn’t just unfold, it unravels you alongside it.

I read this like I was investigating a crime. I’m talking Google Doc open. Notes everywheree. Pausing after chapters just to sit there and process because everything felt like a clue. Every conversation, every glance, every secret tucked between lines, it all meant something.

Dark academia soaked in magic. Murder threaded through mystery. Found family that feels both fragile and inevitable. Bloodlines that don’t just shape identity…but haunt it.

Fae. Vampires. Angels. Sorcerers. Shifters. An academy built for the supernatural and all the chaos that comes with it.

And at the center of it all is our girl Summer.

Summer Tuatha De Daanan, who feels like she’s standing on the edge of something she doesn’t fully understand yet. Misunderstood. Guarded. Searching — for answers, for belonging, for herself. There’s something so quiet about her strength at first, like it doesn’t even realize it’s power yet. And watching that shift? Watching her grow into it? It felt intimate. Earned.

Then there’s Alice… she is fire.

Messy, dangerous, magnetic fire.

The kind that burns too bright and dares you to get close anyway. I loved her instantly, not in spite of her chaos, but because of it. The way she takes Summer in without hesitation, like she already decided you’re mine now, deal with it. And yet, underneath all of that, you can feel the cracks. The things she’s not saying. The parts of her story still waiting to be dragged into the light.

Their dynamic? Unstable. Loyal. Perfect.

Connor Morningstar… I need a moment.

Because if you told me I’d fall for a golden retriever MMC, I would’ve laughed. And yet —here I am. Because Connor isn’t just soft, he’s intentional. Gentle in a way that never feels weak. Patient in a way that never feels forced. He is literally the definition of a golden retriever man.

And yes, their connection is insta love. But it works. It doesn’t feel rushed, it feels inevitable. Like something that was always going to happen the moment their paths crossed. Especially because Summer isn’t naturally soft or open— so watching Connor love her as she is and see her come out of her own emotional cocoon made it hit even harder.

The Morningstar brothers as a whole… that’s family. The kind that feels chaotic and grounding at the same time. The kind that makes you laugh in one moment and then hits you with something unexpectedly soft in the next. Every scene with them just worked.

And then, because this book refuses to let you be comfortable, the headmaster. The Stranger.

A breath of fresh air, but also… something more.

There’s something about the way they interact with Summer that feels layered— like they know more than she does. More than we do. And instead of forcing anything, they’re patient with her. Watching. Guiding. Waiting. And I won’t lie… the energy there? The possibility? Yeah. I’m intrigued. Very intrigued. Especially with our Stranger and the mystery behind who and what he may be.

But now I’m sitting here questioning everything— including who I thought the love interest was supposed to be.

And maybe that’s the point.

This book doesn’t hand you answers. It hands you threads and dares you to follow them without getting lost.

And I did. Happily.

So yeah… I’ll just be here, still thinking about every detail, second-guessing every theory, and impatiently waiting for book two…because I have questions, and this book had the audacity to leave me with all of them.
