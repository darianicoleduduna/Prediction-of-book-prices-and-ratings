THAT ENDING?! The emotional turmoil this book has pulled out of me has been one wild ride. Do not deprive yourself of one of the most iconic ending lines of a book I’ve ever read.. and read The Selvaren Series. Now.

“I marveled at how she found aches hidden inside the tiniest crevices of my being and soothed each one. Her touch was the remedy to every depressing and infuriating thought. Before her, a woman’s touch had been a means to an end, but now? Hers was a necessity; I needed it like I needed air to survive. I hadn’t realized how hollow my life had become until she blazed in and filled it to the brim with love and light.”

The Silversmith already gave us top-tier, torturous yearning, but somehow, this sequel gave us so much more. The desperate levels of love and devotion from the MMC.. the kind I’ve only read about from Kaine & Helena, and Kaan & Raeve—had me feeling so unwell. And the fact we got that same level of yearning and longing from Ary herself in this book has been everything. This book ripped my heart out again and again. This has officially become a god-tier series for me, and there are only two books out.

“Can I touch you?”
“Never ask me that again.”
“Why?”
“Because the answer will always be yes.”

The flashback chapters of young Ary and Gavin falling in love gave me LIFE. I swooned, giggled, and fell in love with these characters even more than I already had. Every glimpse into their past had me feeling so protective of their memories. I held those memories of them in a past life so sacred. Their past was also woven so beautifully into Ary’s present journey of forging her own path and proving herself. And the letters were such an intricate, perfect touch I could honestly cry over almost every single one of them. They added so much more emotional weight to everything Ary was going through. The details of this book AAAHHH. The forehead kisses, her hand on his chest, his sweater, THE RINGS! And that one dream sequence that had me bawling my eyes out! I need the secret wedding scene in the next book please?!

I was already so down bad for Gavin Smyth in book 1, and I genuinely didn’t think it was possible to love him even more. We all know Gavin as the brooding, dark, fiercely protective man who is kind only for her, but getting to see him in his younger years was EVERYTHING. He was so mischievous, roguish, persistent, and completely down bad for Ary since the first time he laid eyes on her. Seeing this playful, lovestruck side of him made me fall for him all over again. I didn’t know I needed young Gavin, but apparently, I very much did. 😭

“You know, you called me crazy once or twice. You always said it in jest, I think, but we both knew it was true. I decided a long time ago that if crazy keeps you safe and ensures you get everything you deserve, I’ll be the craziest motherfucker this world has ever seen.”

But I’d say the romance isn’t the main point of this book.. it’s Ary finally coming into her own and discovering who she really is. She started as a lost, timid, weak, dangerously naive, and insecure prophesied queen. After being lied to and manipulated by nearly everyone around her, this book is where she finally takes everything into her own hands and starts paving her own path. Hell hath no fury like a woman who finally knows who she is and has been scorned. Ary is relentless, strong-willed, fierce, resilient, and so fucking capable of saving herself. Her arc was so empowering and satisfying to read, especially knowing just how vulnerable and lost she was when this story began.

For almost the entirety of the book, I found myself waiting or anticipating for a certain someone to appear and save her whenever she was in danger. And while his absence was absolutely felt during those moments (except, of course, in the flashback chapters), it was also so damn satisfying to watch a woman who was once lost, broken, fragile, and weak become the one who saves herself. Again and again. She didn’t need someone to come and rescue her this time. She became her own rescue.

“The world can collapse around us. The seas can flood the plains. The trees can burn with unquenchable fire. I will stay by your side. And if by the will of the gods you fall into the earth as it crumbles, I will fall with you.”

The other characters are also so much more fleshed out, and the found family tugged at my heartstrings more times than I can count. Damond is HIM. I love him dearly! The twins, Mina and Mavic is such a sweet addition. Mina and Ary’s friendship has grown into something so special, and Mavic has easily become one of my favorite characters in this book, right alongside Shera, of course! Ary’s sassy little Umbra companion who she can actually communicate with. And the fact that Gavin couldn’t bear the thought of Ary being alone and somehow decided she needed a companion, so he gifted Shera to her??? That man. Even when he couldn’t always be there himself, he was still finding ways to make sure she never felt alone. I’m unwell. And then there’s Ary’s betrothed, Elias Winterton. I could definitely see the appeal of his character in the beginning. TBH I was scared of a possible love triangle going into this, since I knew that nothing could ever compare to what the author has established for Ary and Gavin.. that another love interest would have felt so wrong to me, and I would hate it if that actually became the direction of the story. And this book has proven it clearly WAS NOT.

*SPOILERS AHEAD*
Ary has been assaulted. And Elias, after seeing Ary bruised, broken, bleeding to death and his first question was why she had put herself in that situation?! I was FURIOUS. Because of course, somehow, it was always the woman who gets the blame. The scene was so upsetting, and it was honestly difficult to get through. In that moment, Elias went from someone I was beginning to understand and respect to someone who felt completely spineless and deeply conflicted in all the wrong ways.

And the loyalty issue… I see what the author was trying to do—create layered characters instead of making everything black and white. But I still need my characters to have some damn emotional loyalty and boundaries. I don’t care if it’s the man or woman doing it. If you’re supposedly in love with someone, I don’t want to spend an entire book watching you entertain feelings for somebody else, especially after HE waited years for your unloyal ass. Pick a lane!

And with that ending?! WHY couldn’t she just run to Gavin?! I’m already expecting book three to be:

“I want Gavin.”
“But I CAN’T.”
“But I love Gavin.”
“But I CAN’T.”

Repeat for 400 pages. 🙄

She has 11 powers and is basically supposed to be powerful enough to save the damn world, but apparently getting the guy she loves is where her powers hit their limit. Girl can probably defeat an entire army, but Gavin? Absolutely impossible.

At this point, PLEASE just let these two actually be together for most of book three. I want to watch them grow, figure each other out, become a team, and actually develop into the power couple they’re clearly capable of being. Give us the relationship before the last damn chapter! Don’t make us wait 600 pages just to get one crumb of them together at the end.

And honestly, give Commander Fuck Boi a different love interest—someone like Mina—and give them a spin-off. I’d actually read that.
