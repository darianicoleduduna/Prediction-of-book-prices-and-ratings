As owner of her beloved Gingerbread Bakery, Annie Andrews should have a love life to match her business; sugary and sweet. But instead, she’s locked in a game of words with the irritatingly upbeat bar owner down the street.

Mac Sullivan has everything he wants, except the girl he dreams of. It’s easier to argue with Annie than get her to talk to him but with Jeanie and Logan’s wedding coming up, they're about to spend a lot more time together.

As the snowflakes fall and with romance in the air, will Annie see that the one she loves to hate might just be her perfect match after all?

The Gingerbread Bakery is a cozy romantic novel with an enemies to lovers dynamic, small-town setting and a HEA guaranteed!

Laurie Gilmore is a #1 New York Times, Sunday Times, and Globe & Mail bestselling author who writes steamy small-town romance. Her Dream Harbor series is filled with quirky townsfolk, cozy settings, and swoon-worthy romance. The first book in the series, The Pumpkin Spice Cafe, was featured on Good Morning America and was named the TikTok Shop Book of the Year 2024.

She loves finding books with the perfect balance of sweetness and spice and strives for that in her own writing. If you ever wished you lived in Stars Hollow (or that Luke and Lorelai would just get together already!) then her books are definitely for you.

"Because while Annie wasn’t his anymore, he would always be hers."

Annie and Mac came from two very different friend groups in high school. Annie was the 'nerd' and Mac was the 'jock'.

And yet...there was one year where Annie took a leap and Mac followed her. And it was magical.

"Mac’s lips tipped into a tentative smile and Annie was right back in that diner booth trying to convince herself that they didn’t make sense together."

But teenage romances tend not to work out long term. Mac did something unforgiveable to Annie way back then, and even now - years and years later - she refuses to let it go.

However, a wedding is coming up (Janie's!) and both Annie and Mac are in the wedding party...and that means being civil...and even going so far as to work together for the good of the wedding.

The more time Annie spends around this newer, older, and more emotionally mature Mac...the more she realizes that there might just be something to this man.

"Was it remotely possible that the man who had broken her heart all those years ago actually might turn out to be her person?"

Soooo...I have been eagerly anticipating this book for ages now.

Annie and Mac appeared waaaaay back in book 1 and I could absolutely tell that these two belonged together.

And yet...as I read their story...I felt a bit annoyed. I'm not one for books where the characters are mean to each other or circle the same plot point over and over...and this book hit both those tropes.

Annie's stubborn - so stubborn that I started to become frustrated with her. I know Mac spins it as she's steadfast and loving...but at the same time, I did struggle with understanding why he would still want to be with her despite her VERY prickly personality and VERY blatant disregard for him.

With the way she treated Mac, part of me wanted him to just give up on her and move on.

So much of the book circled that same action/reaction (her being mean, him forgiving her and trying to 'prove' he's changed) that I became tired with it and wished the book would just move on.

My annoyances aside, there were elements that I really liked about this book.

As always, the Dream Harbor residents were an absolute delight - no zany town halls in this one but the township still shows up.

I liked seeing Janie's wedding and all of the side characters from previous books...and when Annie and Mac , I did end up enjoying their arc! It just took a lonnnnnng time to get there!

4 stars 🍪

Second-chance romances are usually not my cup of tea, but I really enjoyed this! It definitely wasn't perfect, but it was such an enjoyable, adorable read.

I loved the flashbacks to Annie and Mac’s younger days which were great for providing the necessary history between the pair. But I did feel like it left minimal space for a secondary plot within the present-day chapters outside of simply Annie and Mac reconciling after so many years. The present-day mystery of missing Nana Estelle was fun but I just felt that more could've happened in the present timeline.

Another thing I will say is that I did find myself feeling irritated about how immaturely Annie and Mac handled their situation. It'd been years since their initial rendezvous, and yet they still continued to act as childishly as they did when they first met. The tendency for these grown adults to behave like teenagers was frustrating, and the lack of communication was rather exasperating, but overall I really liked them as a couple, and I loved getting to read about both their past and their present.

Annie’s relentless grudge holding and her reluctance to hear Mac’s side of the story initially really irked me. But after reading what happened between the pair all those years ago, I totally understood why she felt the way she did. The humiliation and heartbreak she felt made me feel so sorry for her and I just wanted to give past-Annie a hug. It was very sweet watching her gradually begin to trust Mac again.

Mac made some poor decisions when he was young and dumb, but I loved how much he yearned for Annie — not only by working hard to gain her forgiveness once he was back in town, but also internally for the years he was away. (And we all love a yearner, right?)

One thing that Laurie Gilmore does really well is that she seamlessly and organically brings all the characters from the previous books in the series together in each subsequent book. I love seeing how each couple has developed since their own book, and this story was no different. I adored how the whole town came together for Jeanie and Logan’s wedding, and also liked how the characters for the next book — Daisy and Elliot — were introduced in a way that felt natural and unforced. This all created the perfect cosy, small-town charm which is what I love the most about this series.

Overall, this was a sweet book. I'm so glad that l finally got to find out the reason for the hostility between Annie and Mac, and I adored watching them become closer again after so many years.

Now, while preparing for Jeanie and Logan’s wedding, they are thrust back together in close proximity. Forced to confront the animosity that has been brewing, their story unfolds through a dual POV in both the present day and flashbacks. This gave real depth to their feelings then and now.

The Dream Harbor backdrop is as lovely as always, and the wacky cast of characters shines again. The whole crew, including the book club, returns ready to celebrate the big town wedding.

Thank you to NetGalley and Harper360 for the advanced copy!
