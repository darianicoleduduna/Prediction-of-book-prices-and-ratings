I knew going into Little Stranger that it was supposed to be a very dark, taboo romance, so I was expecting uncomfortable. But there were several moments where I genuinely had to put the book down and stare into space for a second.

This book is twisted, disturbing, chaotic, and honestly… kind of insane. There were moments where I was intrigued because I wanted to know just how much worse things could possibly get, and then the book would somehow prove me wrong.

The story follows Olivia and Malachi, two foster siblings who grow up together and develop a relationship that becomes increasingly complicated as they get older. The book leans heavily into obsession, trauma, revenge, forbidden attraction, and some VERY questionable choices.

Olivia was honestly a difficult character for me to figure out.

She has been through a LOT, and so much of her personality and decision making seems shaped by everything that has happened to her. She wants control over her own life, but she also spends a lot of the story being pulled around by the people and circumstances surrounding her.

Her relationship with Malachi is where everything gets complicated. On one hand, there is clearly a history between them and a connection that has existed for years. But on the other hand…

These are your foster siblings.

And I KNOW they aren’t biologically related, but the fact that they were raised together as brother and sister made the whole thing feel incredibly uncomfortable to me.
