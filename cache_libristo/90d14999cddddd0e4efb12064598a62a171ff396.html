Orphans. Changelings. Princesses. Queens.
We are all and nothing now, our fate weighed on the scales of the stars and counterbalanced by the forces that oppose us. The shadow of the false king spreads as thick as oil across the kingdom we were born to rule, and none remain untouched by its darkness. Our crowns lay within reach if only we can claim them. All souls are at risk now and when the final battle dawns, a new regime will rise. The time for the true reckoning is upon us and we have proven ourselves as savage as our father, as brutal as our mother, and as wild as the Elements which roar through our veins. We can only hope it is enough. When the last sword strikes the final death in this game of fate and fortune, only one side will be victorious. The Starfall Legion charges at first light. And our destiny rides upon our shoulders as we rush into the end. All hail the True Queens. May our rule last longer than this night.

***This is a dark fantasy romance – don’t go expecting a sweet school for magic with friends around every corner. Fae fight for everything they own and Zodiac Academy is a cutthroat school for students aged 18+ where only the strongest prevail. There’s no Dumbledore here to save anyone’s ass and Lionel Acrux will give Voldemort a run for his money in the evil dictator category. So hold onto your stardust (broomsticks not required) and get ready for a bumpy ride***

So let's get to the tea 👀

The book starts off really well, it was written with heart- not perfect but had heart. I was thoroughly invested for about 400 pages but after that started having major problems. For a book that's over a thousand pages, the lack of clarity and direction was frustrating to read. It is evident that the authors had an ending in mind but they had no clue how to get there. Know what I mean?

With multiple pov coming into place again (not as bad as Sorrow and Starlight), I noticed that the characters personalities were lost in this one. Tory who has been such a strong personality in all these books, seems to have lost her fire. Darcy was herself but idk if anyone noticed but why does she think of Lance as Orion in her pov? Maybe am thinking too much into it. Seth and Caleb thankfully stayed true to themselves, their fans will love this one. I liked Darius, there was a scene with Lance with the gold- cinematic comedy, loved it. Lance is the one who carried this book for me. He was doing everything- action sequences, leading, submitting, assuring, being a romantic. He was himself and I thank the sisters for not ruining him. Max and Geraldine tried, it's okay... Don't get me started on Xavier. The Ruthless Boys of the Zodiac characters made it interesting at times.

The middle, is deeply infuriating to read. The info dumping done with random side quests and unnecessarily delaying the face off with Lionel was not the right execution of this finale. From page 500 to 850, it was a long tedious read for me. The whole thing with Stars and whatnot was a pain to read.

Then comes the final last hundred pages, this is where it picks up a bit and I get swooped into the story again. Blue Lance have my heart. The action sequences were good, not great but good enough. I do admit that I was not satisfied with how things ended with Lionel. Like the dude made everyone hurt, including me and my pocket for investing so much time in this series and that's what he got? Pfft.

The epilogues were sweet and wholesome. I ate them up. But it seems like the authors left plenty of room for a potential next book in the later years? Or maybe it's hinting at a spin off which will be released in a couple of months? I guess we'll find out.

Was this a satisfying end for the series? I loved some of the character arcs at the end, doesn't mean I enjoyed suffering through a thick arse book which could have been 600 pages easy.
Is the series worth it? The characters are worth it. Tho I gotta warn you, it goes downhill from book 7.

Overall, an okayish experience. It's a bittersweet feeling tho.. Am sad to see these characters go for the time being but am glad that it's come to a conclusion at least in some ways. Maybe someday I will have enough patience to reread this series but for now, it's goodbye to Zodiac Academy- I loved you, hated you but you gave me Lance Orion and for that am always thankful 😉 Proud member of the Ori-Hoes 🤭
