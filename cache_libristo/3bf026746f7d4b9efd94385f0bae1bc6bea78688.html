86—EIGHTY-SIX, Vol. 10: Fragmental Neoteny

Asato Asato, Shirabii (Illustrator)

The Republic of San Magnolia...Eighty-Sixth Sector: A lone child soldier, Shinei Nouzen, touches down on a hellish battlefield. As the “Reaper” of the Eighty-Six, he is saddled with the last wishes of his departed comrades, and the obligation to take them as far as he can go. This is the tale of the people who made him into the Reaper, and the cruel, abrupt incidents of death and destruction which stole them from him.

About the author

Asato Asato

52 books257 followers
安里アサト, Asato Asato, born 1985) is a female Japanese novelist. The pen name Asato Asato is a combination of her real name (Toru Asakura) and eighty-eight.
Asato started writing novels just before she entered junior high school.

She initially submitted her work to the Kadokawa Beans Bunko Rookie Award, but when in 2014 her manuscript made it to the third round of the 2014 21st Dengeki Novel Prize, Asato started thinking about writing a novel that was "Dengeki Novel-esque." This novel would become the start of the 86 -Eighty Six- series.

In 2016, 86 -Eighty Six- won the 23rd Dengeki Novel Prize.

Seriously, I’ve never read a series like this one that never fails to disappoint.

DANG.

Where to start?

The backstory in this one. I though Shin was an amazing character before. Before I saw all of the poor kids trauma. Again, dang. It was brutal. You kind of already get the picture that Shin had it hard in the beginning, with his first squadrons but that does not compare with seeing it first hand. It just hit different to see Shin constantly left alone, everyone dying over and over AND OVER again.

So much backstory in this one.

You get the backstory for his scarf, his pistol, Fido (which was awesome), and how he started to carry the names of his comrades. All of it was so EPIC.

It was just so gut-wrenching to see Shin seeing so many tragic things as a child and how he dealt with it.

Something I’ve always appreciated about Shin is how he has a calm personality but he’s always kind and compassionate despite everything he’s been through. And even when he’s taught to kill people (AS A CHILD!!) to put them about if their misery, he still doesn’t lose that humanity part of him.

This one was just… DANG.

Go read it.

So much good character and references and just… ahhhh!!!

(ALSO, it’s no wonder about the imagery/his dreams about all of the previous dead Eighty-Six holding their own heads wiry what happened with Alice. This poor kid.)

Overall this is one I’m sure I’ll have to read again because of how many freaking details and references there are. This is one of those series that’s going to be a classic forever for a reason. In the same way books like The Hunger Games suck you in with their vivid imagery, compelling characters, and relevant social commentary, it’s just one that will always speak the the core of humanity and the struggles we face. Eighty-Six is one with so many parallels and references that I always find something new. It’s awesome.

86 volume 10 written by ASATO ASATO story takes place before the event of book 1 but still follows the main character. This is the 10th book in an ongoing series. Normally the 2 main characters Shinei Nouzen and Vladilena Milizé are in every book. But in this book Vladilena Milizé is missing because so far it takes place before the main story. This book follows the journey of the 86 who are child soldiers through multiple war zones. The book goes over some very controversial issues such as racism, child soldiers, and slavery. This novel is very engaging, funny at some points, and it has a very interesting and complex world.

An example of how engaging this book is in the first battle scene of the book the author starts out every page counting down one number or second. This scene had me on the edge of my seat the whole time. In this scene we see a younger version of the main character in his first engagement with the Legion. The Legion are AI controlled machines made by the Giadian Empire. The entire continent has been in a constant fight with the Legion for over a decade. Each page of the book can be so different because on one they could be laughing and the next they could be dying or about to.

As we saw in the previous paragraph, this book can be exciting and suspenseful. It can also be very comedic at some points due to the main character’s stoic attitude, but unexpected behavior. People who like adventure and deep story elements would love this book. Also there are some romantic elements, especially between the two main characters. Overall, I recommend this book to anyone who loves the thrill of the next page.

One of the most interesting aspects of 86 Eighty-Six has always been our protagonist, Shinei Nouzen. The tenth volume, Fragmental Neoteny, is the prequel to the main story of the series: the people and events that shaped Shin into the Undertaker.
