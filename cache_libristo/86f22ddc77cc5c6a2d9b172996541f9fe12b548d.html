Jeanette Rose, Alexis Rune

Fear. Fury. And Fire.

To Know is Never Enough.

Summer Tuatha De Daanan reluctantly returns to Avalon University for her second year after spending the summer with her vampire best friend in Draculea. She’s a shell of her former self, and the peace promised to her has never felt further from her reach. All Summer wants is to keep her head down and get through the year cloaked in her depression, but looming threats linger, and she quickly finds herself thrust into a dangerous position once again; entangled with a friend turned enemy, the protector of her beloved family, and the target of two very powerful kings.

Unwilling to suffer any further loss, Summer must push through her emotions to prepare herself for the enemies who threaten everything she holds dear. Through runic training with the revered and brooding Headmaster Emrys, combat with the aloof and strong-willed Max, and improving her mental walls with the dark and mysterious stranger, each step pulls her further from the edge of despair. Yet, nothing will be simple for Summer, and the more she trains, the more she discovers who she truly is, forcing her to confront her inner darkness. The beast within that purrs for her enigmatic stalker.

A darkness that is waiting to be explored. A darkness that will not be denied.

There are some stories where the battle is the hardest part. And then there are stories that ask you to survive the aftermath.

After the way Blood & Betrayals ended, I knew returning to this world was going to hurttttt.
I just didn’t realize how deeply.

This book is heavyyyy.

Not because it’s filled with endless action or shocking twists from the first page, but because it chooses to sit with what comes after. After the fall. After the loss.

The grief. The silence. The impossible task of waking up and choosing to keep going after your world has been shattered.

And honestly?
I loved that.
Because grief is not linear.

It lingers. It changes you. It finds you in the quiet moments when you least expect it.
And Summer....

She is a soul character for me.
The kind of character who doesn’t just exist on the page. She settles somewhere inside of you.

Her battles became my battles. Her grief became something I carried alongside her. There were moments where I wanted to cry with her, scream into the void with her, and yes, throw my Kindle across the room because watching her struggle through everything she does felt almost too real.

Jeanette and Alexis deserve all the flowers for creating such a beautifully honest portrayal of grief, healing, and mental health. They don’t rush Summer’s healing or ask her to become “okay” for the sake of the story. They allow her to be angry, broken, hopeful, afraid, and everything in between.

What struck me most is that despite this being a fantasy filled with magic, supernatural beings, and ancient mysteries, the emotions never felt larger than life.

They felt real.
Raw.
Beautifully human.

The magic builds the world.
The emotions make you believe in it.
And because of that, I couldn’t rush this book.

I found myself putting my Kindle down just to breathe, replaying conversations in my head, and simply sitting with everything these characters were carrying before I was ready to continue.

Some books entertain you.
This one asks you to feel everything.

And somehow, even surrounded by heartbreak, the found family remains the heartbeat of this series.

Alice and the Morningstars continue to be everything.
Chaotic. Protective. A little unhinged.
But beneath all of that is soooo much love.

They become Summer’s family in every way that matters. They see her pain, stand beside her through it, and remind her she never has to carry it alone.

And then there’s The Stranger.
This… man? Being? Apparition? Whatever you want to call him, he completely stole my attention in this book.

His patience.
His perseverance.
His unwavering devotion to Summer.
Everything.

He doesn’t try to fix her.
He doesn’t expect her to heal on his timeline.
He simply stays.

And somehow, that quiet devotion says more than grand declarations ever could.

If you thought Blood & Betrayals had you questioning everything…

Just wait.

Because when the twists finally hit?
You’re right back to connecting threads, questioning every conversation, and wondering how you missed what was right in front of you.

Book two didn’t just leave me on a cliffhanger.
It threw me off the cliff and asked me to hang on. And yes, I am STILL spiraling 24 hours later and cannot wait for what the chaos duo does next.

Thank you to Simon Maverick for providing me with an early review copy through NetGalley in exchange for my honest review 🖤

After that ending in blood & betrayals, I just knew this was going to hit hard & it did. My favorite duo has done it again. 😫🖤

It took me a few weeks to write this review. This is the kind of book you sit and reflect on.

Because somehow it felt more raw, more unfiltered. The struggle of trying to get by day by day. The mental health decline, where your darkest thoughts start to take over slowly.

This is about morally complex characters and their navigation through life.

The authors explored trauma, grief, stress, and the portrayal of BPD. As someone who has experienced the impact of BPD through a family member, I would say the authors portrayed it very well. How much it affects your decisions, your mood, and those around you. Acting on impulsive and risky behavior.

At one point, I did have to take a breather because it all felt too personal. But that shows you how well the authors were able to grip my attention.

And if I’m being honest, healing is different for everyone. There’s no limit or deadline.

I felt like I was alongside these characters, wanting to fix everything. I also laughed and rooted for their undying loyalty to each other.

But all seriousness aside, this book remains chaotic, messy, and extremely fun. I’m invested.

Summer is truly a force to be reckoned with, and she deserves all the happiness and love. I also deeply admire her courage and confidence.

Then there’s the Stranger, dangerous and enigmatic but deeply patient. He doesn’t push her; he lets her feel and be herself. His utter devotion to Summer had me gnawing at the bars of my enclosure. If that isn’t yearning, then idk what is.

The found family is everything and still an important piece of the story.

Alice is a ride or die, and seeing her flourish, doing things she didn’t think she was capable of, just shows we can let our walls down with the people who love us the most.

& Rafe. I’m still a Rafe girly through and through. My broody tortured soul. What wouldn’t he do for his family? He’s selfless and kind, and yeah, I love him so much.

The world remains brutal, with threats still lingering in the air and secrets slowly revealing themselves. I’m scared to see what happens next.

The spice? I was sweating. It’s scorching hot. Be ready for lots of cold showers 🥲

I thought I was prepared with my theories, which I was for a few, but then I got sideswiped. I’m still unwell. That cliffhanger obliterated me.

I would say this is character-driven more than anything, and I think it’s crucial for the plot. I’ll be screaming into the void until book 3 because I’m extremely emotionally attached to everyone in here.

Thank you to Simon Maverick, Jeanette Rose, and Alexis Rune for the egalley🖤✨

Summer is still being Summer however and her personality was flat as a potato chip in this book. She’s supposed to be extremely powerful but we don’t see any of that from her this entire book. I wish the plot had progressed just a tad faster in this book for being over 700 pages honestly not much was going on, and the situation with her and the stranger with their back & forth really took over the whole book. It started to become extremely repetitive. Every time they made 1 step forward they took 3 steps back the entire book. The amount of times he told her she was his and her life begins and ends with him was a little overkill. We had about 4 main events in 700 pages but this book felt like we made zero plot progression.

That being said I adore these characters, so yes while I felt like not much happened I still enjoyed every second of my time with these characters while reading this book. I feel like readers who are invested in these characters, and like a book that reads like a CW show you will love this book, but if you’re a reader that likes world building, magic, and an in depth plot this is not going to be for you.
