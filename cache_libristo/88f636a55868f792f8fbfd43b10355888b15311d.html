I can't wait to see them parent and reconcile.

I’m being generous with this rating bc the beginning fooled me 😭. I was gearing up for what could have been my favorite read of this year, but I ended up SO dissatisfied. The potential was there, the setup had me excited, and I genuinely thought I was about to eat this book up. Unfortunately, things went downhill, and by the end, I was left wondering what exactly went wrong.

°ᡣ𐭩.°.જ⁀➴ Enzo 🫶🏽

Enzo was amazing. Wish he had a little more self-respect, but whatever. I love his personality, he carried this book, but there’s only so much he could do 😭. He was hilarious and genuinely had me laughing out loud. I looked forward to his moments because he actually had a personality that made me want to keep reading.

He’s too good to be that loser, I’m sorry. He has the personality, the looks, the skills, like he’s PERFECT. And yet here he is putting up with Tom’s nonsense. I needed him to stand up a little more because why are you giving this man so many chances when he keeps giving you reasons not to? 😭

Honestly, Enzo deserved better. I loved him so much, and I wish the story had given him a relationship that made me as happy as his character did. He was carrying the entertainment, and I needed the romance to meet him halfway.

°ᡣ𐭩.°.જ⁀➴ Tom 🙄

Now onto that loser Tom. I knew I didn’t like his ass from Twisted Pawn bc he was awful there too. And somehow, he managed to continue being awful here. This man keeps doing terrible things and doesn’t even apologize for them, yet Enzo forgives him, and that irritated me SO BAD.

Like, sir??? Can we get some accountability around here? Why is Enzo doing all this forgiving when Tom can barely be bothered to acknowledge what he did wrong? I don’t mind flawed characters, but I need them to actually recognize their flaws and make an effort to do better. You can’t just keep hurting someone and expect everything to be fine because you have a sad backstory.

His personality is nothing to speak of. He’s a brick wall. Not even grumpy, just a miserable old bitch 😭. I genuinely struggled to understand what Enzo saw in him beyond the obvious attraction because personality-wise, this man was giving me NOTHING.

And I really didn’t care for his “heartbreaking” background. I know we’re supposed to understand him better because of what he’s been through, but unfortunately, I just wasn’t moved. A tragic past doesn’t automatically make me sympathize with a character, especially when they’re actively making everyone else’s life miserable. At some point, I need more than a sob story, sir.

My BIGGEST pet peeve with this book is him calling Enzo “kid.” WTF IS THAT ABOUT??? 😭 Even in the middle of sex, btw. That’s weird to me. Like, please, read the room. You’re supposed to be having a romantic moment, and that’s what you choose to call him??? I cannot.

Plus, he had a pet name for him, so why didn’t he just use it? Enzo’s wasn’t any better either, but whatever. 💀 I was already struggling with Tom, and then this man kept calling him kid and making it worse for himself.

I fear I am officially Tom’s biggest hater because nothing this man did could convince me to like him. Enzo, baby, please stand up. 🫩

★ - ̗̀⇢. Overall thoughts

The book wasn’t moving, but it wasn’t terrible either. I just expected SO much more, especially because the beginning had me convinced I was going to love it. The story had potential, but the execution didn’t work for me, and I found myself getting increasingly frustrated as I kept reading.

The smut was lackluster, the plot was meh, and the romance just didn’t give me what I wanted. Which is especially disappointing because I was looking forward to these two, but the chemistry and relationship weren’t enough to make up for everything else I disliked.

I did love seeing Achilles and Tierney again, so a whole star for them 🙂‍↕️. I love them sm, and seeing them again was genuinely one of my favorite parts of the book. They were a welcome addition because at least I had something to be excited about 😭.

Overall, I’m disappointed because I really thought this was going to be a favorite, but it just wasn’t for me.

⁀➴ ♡ Romance ⁺˖ ⸝⸝

˚₊‧꒰ ❝ ⌗ This man's hands did terrible things. Too bad I still wanted them all over me. ❞ ࠬ ׅ ໑ ₊

I really wanted to love this romance. A forbidden, age-gap relationship with a dark, obsessive edge is EXACTLY my kind of thing. While those elements are technically there, the execution left me wanting so much more. I understand Tom and Enzo have history, but this book is meant to work as a standalone. If you haven't read the previous books, I don't think that history translates particularly well into the relationship we're presented with here. Instead of feeling like two people gradually developing feelings for each other, their progression felt uneven and rushed for a good portion of the first half. Because WHY was I less than 100 pages in and already reading internal monologues suggesting they had serious feelings for each other? Considering their dynamic, I expected more resistance, tension, and a slower build. I wanted to feel the push and pull of a relationship that probably shouldn't happen, not feel like I'd skipped several chapters of emotional development. The foundation for a genuinely compelling romance was there, but the pacing didn't give it the room it needed to hit as hard as it could have. And then we have the pet names. Usually, I'm not particularly bothered by them, but Tom calling Enzo "kid" was something I just couldn't get past. I know there's an age gap, and Enzo is less experienced than Tom, but there's a time and place for it. Why are we calling someone "kid" in the middle of an intimate moment? It completely took me out of the scene, and eventually, I was speed-reading through some of their intimate moments because I felt so uncomfortable. Not exactly the reaction I want to have when reading a dark romance. Another thing I couldn't ignore was how it felt like every time they were intimate, they would break up immediately afterward. Like, damn, did the post-nut clarity hit that hard? Because the emotional whiplash was becoming a little ridiculous. Despite all of this, I don't think they're a bad match. In a weird way, I actually think they're cute together, and there were a few moments that genuinely made me giggle or smile. Those moments were too few and too far between for me to get invested in their relationship the way I wanted. I also really enjoyed the handful of scenes where they simply sat down, talked, and allowed themselves to be open with each other. Those moments felt softer, more emotionally intimate, and honestly more romantic to me than some of their physical encounters. I wish we'd gotten more of that. One thing I was also side-eyeing was the way mental illness was approached in their relationship. Recognizing something in your partner and immediately thinking, "Hey, I have that too!" doesn't necessarily feel like the healthiest foundation for a relationship, at least not without more reflection and understanding. I would have appreciated a little more nuance rather than that realization feeling like a shortcut to connection. Ultimately, I think Tom and Enzo had the potential to be a really compelling couple. I just never felt their relationship fully lived up to that potential. The ingredients were there; the execution simply wasn't consistent enough for me.

Enzo was the saving grace of this book. He's immature, chaotic, and definitely not always the most sensible person in the room, but at least he's entertaining! He brought a personality to the story that I wanted more of, and he was one of the few things that kept me invested when the rest wasn't working for me. I also appreciated the moments when we got to see a softer, more vulnerable side of him, especially when he and Tom were actually communicating instead of getting caught up in their usual cycle. Those glimpses made him feel more layered and helped me see the potential in their relationship, even when I wasn't entirely sold on it.

﹕ ꒰ა Thomas Rothwell .ᐟ ֹ ₊

I don't know if it's because I've read so much dark romance this year, but the tall, dark, and broody MMC just isn't doing it for me anymore. At least, not when there's little else to make him stand out. Tom felt like a recycled character I've read a hundred times before. He's the brooding, intimidating man with a softer side for the person he loves, and while I can absolutely enjoy that archetype, I need something more to make it memorable. For most of this book, I struggled to find that something with Tom. I didn't necessarily dislike him; I just didn't have many strong feelings about him, which, arguably, is worse. That said, I did appreciate how gentle he could be with Enzo. Those moments gave him a little more dimension and showed there was more to him than the usual dark-romance exterior. I just wish we'd gotten more opportunities to explore that side of him and understand him as a person beyond his relationship with Enzo.

⁀➴ ♡ Writing and Plot ⁺˖ ⸝⸝

My biggest issue here is that the story felt like it was missing a substantial plot outside of the romance. Now, I understand that this is a romance book, and I'm not expecting every page to be filled with mafia politics and criminal activity. But given the world this series takes place in, I expected the mafia elements to play a bigger role. Instead, we get very little of that side of the story, and the moments involving mafia business often seem more focused on other characters than on Tom and Enzo themselves. I hate constantly comparing this book to the previous installments, but it's hard not to when those books gave me more of what I expected from this series. Here, the story felt almost like a filler episode in a TV show: enjoyable enough in places, but primarily focused on setting things up for the next couple rather than giving this particular story enough substance of its own. The pacing didn't help, either. The first half dragged for me, and I struggled to get invested in what was happening. Then the second half finally picked up, and suddenly I was enjoying myself much more. I don't know what it is about this series, but I'm starting to notice a pattern: I struggle through the first half of these books, only for the second half to completely win me over. It's a very confusing reading experience, and I'm not sure whether to be impressed by the turnaround or frustrated that it takes so long to get there.

⁀➴ ♡ Final Thoughts ⁺˖ ⸝⸝

I really, REALLY want to continue this series. I enjoy being in this world, and there's clearly something about these characters and their stories that keeps pulling me back in. Unfortunately, Fallen Rook didn't quite live up to my expectations, especially considering how excited I was to read it. I spent a good chunk of this book feeling disappointed, and while the second half improved my overall experience, it couldn't erase my issues with the pacing, the romance, and the lack of a stronger plot. I can see the potential in Tom and Enzo's relationship without pretending I was completely captivated. Still, I'm glad I stuck with it instead of DNF-ing and letting that first half determine my entire opinion. The turnaround was real, even if it took some struggle to get there. At least the next book is about Luca and Sofia, and I'm genuinely looking forward to their story. PLEASE let this one be the book that has me hooked from beginning to end. Fingers crossed!
