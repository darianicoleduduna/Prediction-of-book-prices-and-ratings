Ari Alvarez,




a recent nursing graduate student who is no stranger to the military lifestyle. She's naive, fearful yet motivated after the passing of her older brother. She's entering a new chapter in her life where she has to adjust without her best friend and start a career that mentally and physically challenges her. I tried, I really did but this book is struggling for more than one reason.
1. It needed way more editing. There is basic grammatical errors, poor sentence structure, and repeated phrases on almost every page. I’m not joking when I say that virtually every piece of dialogue is not punctuated correctly. Some scenes don’t even make sense, (her mom and her are at a coffee shop, then go grocery shopping, and back to a coffee shop?)
2. The writing is very immature. There’s no depth to what I’m reading beyond a good line every now and then. It reads like a 15 year old’s diary, not as a 22 year old trauma nurse going into the Army.
3. This book is directionally lost. There’s no consistency to the character’s arcs. You have a MMC you’re marketing as dark and dangerous, like Ghost and yet this guy seems like a wet rag, totally normal dude for a quarter of the book? There’s nothing eye catching or interesting about Danny when they meet, or gives you the feeling that he’s some tortured soul. "I also just want to be a part of the best team in the world and kill bad guys. Simple. As. That." That’s an actual line of his.
4. This book is not interesting in the slightest for the 22% that I read. She’s already about to sleep with him at 12% which made no sense considering she’s a virgin and had known him for 2 days. And then surprise! He’s actually BFFs with her brother who passed but we’ll just randomly throw that in there.

I had no interest in reading more about Danny and Ari because of the lack of chemistry.

This book way over promised and under delivered. I understand it’s a debut, but there’s a line between an author being new and not yet perfecting their craft, and a book that needed heaps of work before it should’ve been published. This is the latter.

Easy 5 stars!! I absolutely loved this book!

This is a dark, military romance. There are LOADS of trigger warnings. However, as far as the darkness scale. It’s probably in the middle in my opinion. This wasn’t too intense for me, and I get squeamish sometimes with dark romance.

The FMC, Ari, is a military nurse who is grieving the loss of her brother, a Navy seal that died the same year. She meets our MMC, Danny, a morally grey Navy seal who has something to do with her brother’s death. Danny is also super well known in the military for killing tons of people, including 3 of the top most wanted terrorists, which has landed him the nickname “Grim Reaper.”

As the story goes on, Ari is also being stalked and threatened by her abusive ex and is trying to deal with falling hard for Danny and the struggles of being in a relationship while working in the military.

This definitely has some “touch her and you die vibes.” He’s very protective and obsessed with her, but his character development takes a bit longer.

Some things you can expect:

-They get spicy in a helicopter. LOVE that
