In the mafia world, women don’t reign.

I have a legacy to protect, a power to snatch, and no one will stop me.

To win, I’ll risk it all.

Rina Kent is a New York Times, USA Today, and #1 bestselling author of all things dark romance.
Better known for writing unapologetic anti-heroes and villains, Rina weaves tales of characters you shouldn’t fall for but inevitably do. Her stories are laced with a touch of darkness, a splash of angst, and just the right amount of unhealthy intensity.

The heroine: Rai (Raina Sokolov) - She is the Grand-daughter of the former Bratva’s Pakhan, and now the Grand-niece of the current one. She wants more power herself but the Bratva is a man’s world and they don’t allow women. Though in the time since Kyle left and her Babushka died, she has gained a bit of her own power.

The Love Story: When Kyle left seven years ago it nearly broke Rai and she has become a different person since then. She is upset when the Bratva takes Kyle back so easily after he basically left them. Nobody leaves the mafia. In order to save her innocent niece from an arranged marriage, Rai takes her place, but when she agreed, she had no idea the groom would be Kyle.



This is a dark mafia romance where the bride is stuck in an arranged marriage against her will. The groom has a very shady past and she doesn’t trust him at all. The fact that she knew him well in the past, trusted him and was attracted to him before he left and broke her heart, makes her that much more acrimonious. Though Kyle wants his new wife, it seems he might just be toying with Rai while having another agenda completely.

The arranged mafia marriage is a trope used often in dark romance, but it is one I continue to like. However, the misogynistic mafia world is often hard to take, but I genuinely liked Rai and Kyle. Though some of the things Kyle did weren’t great. Especially getting crazy jealous when she even talked to another guy. It is one thing in a book to show jealousy but another to tell a woman she can’t even talk to another guy when she isn’t flirting with the guy(s).

I love Mafia books and I love arranged marriage tropes. Add a bit of hate and angst and you got one of my favorite types of romance.

If you have not read the Truths and Lies duet by Rina - read that first. This duet spoils that storyline a lot and it would be a shame for it to be known without reading Rai's twin sisters story and how the hell it all came down.

Kyle left 7 years ago and Rai never really forgave him. He was her bodyguard and just never came back one day, no phone calls, no letters, nothing. It wasn't like these two were in a relationship or had any sort of emotional connection that this would be a bf or a lover fucking off. Nope. She did like him and found him attractive but that's as far as it went between them. She was 21 and he was 28. He was only her guard for 2 years and helped her deal with the issues concerning her sister’s safety.

I'll be honest and say Kyle isn't my favorite MMC from Rina. How can he be when he was sharing a bunch of scenes with Adrian aka the god of MMC's (in my eyes). We know he is back after all those years to get revenge on the Irish and the Russians, but you don't really know why and what prompted this change. It's however not difficult to imagine it might have to do with his parents and his childhood.

He weasels his way in and gets Rai to agree to an arranged marriage between them and attempts to impregnate her for some reason even though in his POV he is constantly thinking about leaving after his mission is finished.

Slowly but surely these two find common ground and have explosive sex. Kyle becomes OTT and obsesses with Rai. We don't know much about what he was up to in those 7 years of absence, but I wish we had gotten a glimpse?

Neither are virgins. Rai lost it according to her 10 years ago (to someone we don't know) and since then has not been with anyone and it's not because she was sad about Kyle leaving but mainly because she never felt like she needed/wanted sex or wanted guys. She was too focused on being part of the Bratva and making herself important in the world of the misogynistic men around her.

Obviously, things go to shit once these two get a bit of peace between each other. Rai overhears Kyle on the phone plotting the Bratva's demise and saying Rai means nothing...someone attacks her and throws her down the stairs.

We get a good chunk of insights about Kirill and his bodyguard and Rai knows something about them but we don't know what yet.

Also, every time Adrian was mentioned or in the scenes it made me smile. I love him and any glimpses of him makes me automatically happy. We got some Lia scenes, and you realize how deep everyone believes Adrian doesn't love his wife.

The end of this book leaves you with more questions then answers.
Why is Kyle trying to destroy the Russians along with the Irish when the Irish/Russian do not get along?
Who hurt Rai at the end?
What will Rai do now that she knows Kyle has been lying and plotting?
Betrayal in the Bratva is punished with death – so how will Kyle get out of that?
What’s the story behind Kirill and his bodyguard?
