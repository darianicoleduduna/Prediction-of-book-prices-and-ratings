They say love makes you foolish. At Bloodwing, it makes you dangerous.

I thought surviving a vampire academy was the hardest thing I’d ever have to do.

Then I fell in love with a boy who can turn into a dragon.

After the events in Veilmar, my only concern is for Blake. Will he survive the monster who possesses him? But as I fight a battle for the soul of the man I love, my dearest friend is up against the greatest danger a dragon rider can ever face.

Meanwhile, as Bloodwing reels from loss and betrayal, an ancient prophecy tightens its grip, and an unexpected triad is rising—one powerful enough to change the balance of power in Sangratha forever.

As the Veil cracks and the dead come back to haunt us, love becomes a weapon—sharp enough to destroy the world or strong enough to forge a new one.

This dark fantasy series tackles mature themes. Readers are advised to consult the trigger warnings before embarking on this intense, morally gray/black journey through the realm of Sangratha!

Sooo… why did we need this 4th book? Was it just so we could have unnecessary, cringey, spice scenes? Cause it sure felt like that.

We should have stopped while we were “semi” ahead… but alas, here we are.

The dialogue between characters was so incredibly cringey and unnatural. To begin with, I viewed this series as just junk food. It was addicting, at least the first two books, but it wasn’t good. The third book felt like it lost its way… and this last book was just junk in my opinion.

People change emotions and come to conclusions WAY too quickly. Things conveniently happen to a comical degree. People don’t talk like normal people. So much over-explaining and saying everything that is on their minds without any prompt or reasoning behind it.

Also, can we talk about how when Medra and Nyxaris were fighting about Florence and he kept saying “she is my rider” and Medra never said that once, but then he snapped at her and said, “stop calling her just my rider.” … Yeah, this type of thing happened a lot where someone didn’t say something, or things were misremembered in the text… it was SO odd…

Things are sprung up out of nowhere with no steps to make it make sense or to get there. Kage was ruined, what he and Regan had was ruined. It was cringey, preachy, and just gross. Can we also talk about how there was ZERO foreshadowing at all for Kage and Lysander? Where in the world did that come from? Again, only in to have a “Why Choose” relationship…

Blake was the only one, and I say this lightly, that I enjoyed reading his dialogue for. I thought his banter and humor were great to see. But also… he didn’t feel at all like the Blake we knew in the series.

The ending “fight”, if you can even call it that because we didn’t really get a fight or have any stakes at all, was anti-climatic and WAY too easy. They just fell into their laps, and a paragraph later the bad guys are dead… 4 books and that is what we got? I get they have already been through a lot, but why did we need a whole other 600 page book to get THAT? It should have ended as a trilogy, I would have accepted it better.

I honestly believe we only got this book so we can have a lot of unnecessary sex scenes to get people “excited”. That was all this book was. And to see a really strange threesome.

Florence finds Nyxaris in human form? Immediately sex, “I love you”… huh… well they didn’t waste any time.

Regan can’t handle physical touch? But don’t worry, after she sleeps on it, she’s ready for a threesome in the morning!

I felt nothing while reading this book, no remorse for any of the deaths, no reactions to any of the “twists”, why did we need this book? Nothing but a disappointment, introducing developments out of nowhere and just cringey. I wasn’t expecting a lot, because again this series is just junk food for me, but I felt like we could have gotten more than THIS.

2.75 - ARC REVIEW

Firstly, thank you so much to Briar Boleyn for the opportunity to read an ARC of With Hearts of Flame. All opinions are completely my own.

I already know this may be a slightly controversial take, and based on the amount of 4 and 5 star reviews I’ve seen, I may very much be in the minority here. I genuinely hate giving this 2.75 stars because I’ve really enjoyed the Bloodwing Academy series, but unfortunately this finale just didn’t hit the way I expected it to.

After reading the prequel series and bridging novella, I was expecting an epic, emotional finale with huge battles and massive payoff. There were moments I enjoyed, but overall I finished feeling underwhelmed.

My biggest issue was that there simply wasn’t enough Medra and Blake. For the final book in their series, I wanted so much more focus on them, but with so many characters, POVs and storylines, they sometimes felt pushed to the side.

I also loathe to admit it…but I needed more Viktor. I really think his POV would have added so much more context and emotional weight. There was also a pretty significant moment involving Flo that I really wish we had experienced from her POV, rather than through another character.

Flo and Nyxaris felt a little strange to me at the beginning too, personally, some of it just felt too soon.

The “power triad” storyline unfortunately wasn’t for me either.

The journey to the dwarven city gave me slight Lord of the Rings vibes. Kazmyn also gave me Ridoc from Fourth Wing vibes, almost like he was there to be the funny member of the quest squad, but unfortunately a lot of the humour felt a little forced to me rather than naturally funny.

Rodriguez, however,absolute twat. Some very weird behaviour from that man.

I was glad to see Hawl again, and the mention of Lancelet being dropped somewhere else after she was meant to follow Hawl, immediately made me suspicious because…why mention her now?! Possible spin-off setup?

My biggest disappointment, though, was the emotional payoff. Some deaths happened incredibly quickly, while others felt almost skimmed over when I thought those characters deserved far more.

The ending should have destroyed me. I understood the prophecy and why everything happened, but I just didn’t feel there had been enough build-up for the sacrifice to hit emotionally the way it should have.

Then the epilogue introduced a trope that, personally, I really don’t think was needed.
There were still things I enjoyed,. I love this world, and I enjoyed seeing connections to the wider series, but after everything leading into this finale, I expected it to feel epic.Instead, I finished thinking......is that it?

I’d still recommend the Bloodwing Academy series, but for me this finale needed more emotional fallout and a much bigger payoff.

📖 Review:
I can’t believe this series is over!

First, I have to say how happy I am with the way things have played out for Blake and Medra throughout this entire series. I love these two so much, and seeing where they ended up made my heart happy. This was a wonderful conclusion to an already amazing series, and I’m genuinely sad to say goodbye to these characters.

Now, I went into this book SO excited for more Florence. I know she’s technically a side character, but let’s be honest… she’s NOT just a side character to me. She was the person I was most excited to see more of, and I really enjoyed the direction her storyline took. That said, I did feel like things moved a little too quickly. I would have loved to see her story unfold more gradually and have more time to watch her relationship develop.

And then there’s Regan. Oh, Regan. I am SO happy with the direction the author took her character. We’ve gotten to see so much growth from her throughout this series, and I absolutely loved seeing that.

I also really enjoyed getting a little crossover from the Blood of Fae series! I was excited to see those connections, although I will admit I was hoping to see a few more familiar faces. Maybe that’s just me wanting ALL my favorite characters in one place.

Overall, I thought this was a good ending to an amazing series. While there were a few things I wish had been given a little more time and attention, I’m happy with where things landed. It’s always bittersweet finishing a series you’ve loved, and I’m so excited to see what this author comes up with next!


🎧 Audiobook thoughts:
THE AUDIOBOOK WAS FANTASTIC!

The narration kept me completely locked in, and I had such a great time listening. Everyone did a wonderful job, and I honestly couldn’t have asked for a better audiobook experience.

The final Bloodwing Academy books had a lot to accomplish and to wrap up. The end of the third book left us in absolute chaos with the city in ruins, Florence and Regan captured, and Blake running off in despair after a botched attempt to rid himself of his dragon.

I enjoyed the call back to Medra’s beginnings and the tie in to Briar’s related series. I enjoyed Regan’s redemption and the triad that formed between her, Kage, and Lysander. It was interesting to get to see the Dwarves more and to learn about their part in all of this.

Critically, I think that this book did what a lot of fantasy series’ do – rush the ending to wrap everything up neatly. I do wish that the series would have been plotted out more ahead of time to help the pacing at the end. Blake’s eventual love of Medra was such a quick 180 turn around and I would have liked to spend some more time with that struggle.

I adore Briar’s writing and I think that this is overall a five-star series. However, this wasn’t my favorite book in the series and I found my attention wandering quite a bit.
