If I would rate a book based on emotional investment then I'd give each book in this series 5 stars easily. The way I'm fucking OBSESSED with some characters (I think you know who I'm talking about) is beyond healthy and they deserve all the stars, BUT the keyword here is 'some'.

What started out as DUAL POV in ZA: The Awakening (Darcy & Tory) turned into a 12 POV in Heartless Sky and not to sound disrespectful, but I just don't care that much about half of these POVs, especially the Lionel, Xavier or Geraldine's ones to name my least favs. I understand that Lionel's POV are there to see his humiliation and downfall from his eyes, which should have given me some kind of satisfaction, but it did not. We just switched between way too many POVs when I just wanted the focus to be one the main crew. Additionally, I feel like too many POVs are distracting from the main plot and if you switch up the perspective, it should be to make a point, give us some interesting insight, or make an impact to the story, which it did not for the majority of the POV switches.

Also, I never had a problem with the lengths of any of these books and most of the time I didn't feel like they were that long, because this series is so easy to read, but I felt the length in this book due to all the unnecessary filler chapters from characters I couldn't care less about.

Lastly, what the first 85% of this book added to my life, the last of 10% of this book gave me a cardiac arrest.
