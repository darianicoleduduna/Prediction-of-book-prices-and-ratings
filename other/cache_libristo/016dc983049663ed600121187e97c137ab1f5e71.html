↠ DARCY VEGA.

This girl amaze me. I always loved Darcy in the series. Throughout the series she was the person that I could relate the most too, but wow in this book she has proven times and times again that she is worthy of being the queen Solaria needs.

Darcy has been through some rough shit throughout this series (they all have been), but it never changed her personality and who she fundamentally is. Despite the pain, the horror, the torture that she has seen and witnessed herself, she always stayed true to herself. All the pain made her stronger instead of more jaded or bitter. She stays kind. She sticks to her morals and she believes in second chances.

Basically the majority of this book Darcy spends without power + the Shadow Beast inside of her, because of the curse Lavinia put on her. She kept fighting for dominance of the Shadow Beast who wants to control her body and while she doesn’t always win the fight, she never gives up and always stands back up. She was such a badass in this book and told everyone off who was in her way (Lionel, Stella, Lavinia etc.) even without her powers. Darcy never backs down and I just love her so much. 😭

But the scene that truly impressed me beyond anything was when Darcy was finally free of the Shadow Beast. When she fought the Shadow Beast, she could have killed it. The chance was right there and she did not. Instead she used her powers to heal the Beast and free it from Lavinia’s shackles. No one would have blamed her for wanting to kill this Beast that brought her so much heartache and pain. Something that took all of her powers and used it against her and everyone she loved. A monster that used her body to kill and hurt other people during and after the battle. Instead she saw beyond that and knew this was just another animal who was deeply wounded and in captivity and gave it a second chance. This scene with Darcy and the Shadow Beast is not even emotional, but just thinking about it makes me want to cry. 🥺

It was slower at moments, but it allowed us to absorb everything that happened at the end of Heartless Sky, and it allowed the characters to try to come to terms with their loss and grief. After that, though, it got so long and repetitive, and after the 50% mark, I just lost interest in most of the plot and wanted it to be over. Thankfully, the last 20% were better, with more action and relevant things happening, but it still was not enough to save this book from my low 2.5-star rating.

The smut felt forced at times. I do enjoy smut, but the context and mood are important too. I understand that Darcy and Orion are mates and that they do not know if they will make it out of the palace, but them having sexy times in the cage they are being held in, in the middle of the throne room where anyone can enter at any time, and after Orion has been tortured and healed again is just weird. It didn’t feel romantic, or even desperate; it just felt as if they were two horny faes that couldn’t control their hormones… Or as if the authors wanted to add smut just for the sake of having spicy scenes in the book, even if it didn’t fit with the mood and context. But maybe that’s just me being too picky.

That being said, I am still in love with Darcy and Orion, and I hope with all my heart that they will get their HEA. Their intensity, loyalty, and fierce love are everything. Tory, on the other hand, disappointed me. She started well, and her feelings were very relatable, but she ended up being selfish, as she tended to be in the previous books. Ican't judge her actions because I have no idea what she is going through, but the fact remains that she would have been willing to sacrifice everyone and everything (except Darcy) to achieve her insane plan to get Darius back. Even her friends.

The scenes with Leon and Dante were just amazing. I missed Leon’s silliness and their interactions… It made me want to reread the Ruthless Boys series. The part where they are building a giant nest to grab Gabriel’s attention so he doesn’t have visions of the rebels, but only has visions of the nest instead, made me smile so much. It’s a typical crazy-but-kind-of-brilliant Leon plan. I am sad that we didn’t see more of Gabriel, though; I feel that his character’s potential is being wasted…

While I immensely enjoyed the moments with the Ruthless boys, other chapters were simply annoying and frustrating. Why have so many POVs? I don’t really care about Justin, Vard, or Mildred. I don’t need or want chapters from their POVs, and let’s be honest, it’s not as if their chapters were relevant.
