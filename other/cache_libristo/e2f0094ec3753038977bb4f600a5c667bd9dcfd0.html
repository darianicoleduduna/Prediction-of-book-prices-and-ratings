***5 STARS***

“I’m not protecting you because you’re a woman. I’m protecting you because you’re my woman.”

I didn’t think it was possible to top Book #1 but Rina Kent did. This book was the EPIC conclusion to the perfect dark billionaire romance.

I won’t go to much into the storyline to avoid giving away spoilers but this book was expertly written. It was dark but not disturbingly dark. Jonathan King was the textbook possessive and controlling protector of Aurora. As he says he would protect her from anyone, including her. And he did. Dominant, firm, and pure alpha Jonathan King rules his empire with a firm hand and it was beautiful to see his feelings for Aurora break through that tough facade. When she needed him he was always there loving and protecting her patiently and with an indomitable love.

I didn’t want this book to end and the author gave us a phenomenal epilogue to see how one of my favorite couples fares in life.

I highly recommend this book!

The biggest problem I had with this book was the ending. I guessed who the bad guy was pretty quick, but it wasn't necessarily obvious who that person was. I also didn't care for the entire loose ending with the heroine's dad. It's a bit unbelievable that we went though two books of her past being such a big part, just for it to end that way with no aftermath. Same with Alicia's death.
