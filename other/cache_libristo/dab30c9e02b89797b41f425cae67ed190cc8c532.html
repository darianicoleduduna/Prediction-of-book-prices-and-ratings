Me when I saw two Jacks on my screen:



I MISSED MYSHAYLAAAAAAS EVAJACKS SM!!!!

“She didn't know what it was like when she wasn't there. The Sun doesn't know the night because it brings its own light. And to Jacks, Evangeline was the Sun.”

Not this book dragging me out of the clutches of my reading and review slump. If anyone can do it, it's definitely Miss Stephanie Garber. To be very honest, I don't think there is a single book by her that I have read and not rated 5. Some of the authors are like the ones who click for you instantly, and she's like that because I just get consumed each and every time she puts out something. I remember I finished each of the books in the Caraval series within the span of 24 hours, and even less for OUABH because no matter how much I try to, like, savor it, I just can't help but inhale them 😭 With this, I tried my best to savor it because I think it's been around three years since I last read her book and I MISSED IT SO DAMN MUCH!! So, I kept telling myself, 'no, no, no, no, no, you gotta savor it.'

I legit cannot express in words how beautiful it was to read about my beloveds again 😭❤️‍🩹 I love how perfectly whimsical these books are. 250 pages are so less!! I wanted more, mainly because I got lost in the world, and can you guys blame me? The way this woman writes, it's like even if you don't want to leave this world, the descriptions will force you to jump into this world. It gave me everything that I wanted. I wasn't expecting much from this story tbh. Even if I got, like, just EvaJacks hanging around the hollow, I would've slapped 5 stars and went on with my way, but instead I was given more, an actual story that was so beautiful!!

Also, I am not going to lie, I have always wanted more from EvaJacks because the way ACFTL concluded, it made me feel like there could have been more, and I was skeptical, like, teeny tiny bit, that she might ruin something between them, but boy, as soon as I read the very first page, I knew I would love the fuck outta this book. The way she integrated the whole Mirror of Infinite Endings plot here and how it was sort of like a she would always for him and vice versa thing was so damn cute, esp since it was a test and all. I wasn't expecting that from the story, but I was so glad that she took it that way. It made me so nostalgic because it felt like I was back doing my A levels, skipping Econ and CS lectures to read OUABH in the Library. This type of nostalgia>>> I needed to read this book so badly man 😭

Finally, onto my pookie wookies Evangeline, MY PINK HAIRED DIVA who was so damn cute 🥹 like where can I get a woman like that 😭 she was so so so perfect, I loved how dramatic she was at times with a touch of her being a teeny bit naive, she was also so charming. Her being a dragon mommy was not on my bingo card. Her with Bruce was something else 😭 She had me on the edge of the seat when she went through the mirror because you honestly cannot expect how things are going to go when she's in a situation like that. I also liked that she did not fuss about the main reason for Jacks's deal and her reaction was so understanding and warm that I LOVEDD IT!!! Her beauty definitely lies with how she makes her life so whimsy and magical. AND Jacks, MY APPLE EATING DIVA, I loveeeed how he was all broody and scowly at everyone but all smiles and giggles with Eva 😭 this mf didn't even leave Bruce alone with the scowling pls 😭 but his love and obsession for Eva was so 🫦 like I get you Jacks. Him trying to find ways to spend his entire life with Eva, and also being super stressed about everything because nothing ever goes as planned for Jacks 😭 he was charming too, but I wish he munched on apples a bit more. Honestly, I'm glad we got a bit of the old jacks through the mirror because it was so needed, and I missed it.

Now onto the two character that absolutely stole the show for me!! Chaos&Lala, like I'm not even joking, I need their book! I have been yearning for it since 2023. Them in this book were something else. The chemistry was off the charts. I just love how it was a set up for their own book, which I am so sure we are going to get!! But I love how Stephanie carried their characters without them overshadowing EvaJacks, it was so fullfilling and I can NOT wait for their book.

Romantically, this book was everything. I will take even crumbs of the domestic/yearning scenes of EvaJacks that we get over anything smutty. All of it so damn precious. WE WERE FED!! The mirror plotline just added more and more to it, which is why I loveeeeeee the addition of it 😋 but honestly, no one comes close to them, their chemistry and banter are off the charts, it's like you can't read about them and not giggle or blush. I think it came to a point where my feet were about to kick 😅 If you crave the fulfilling romance that was needed after ACFTL, Definitely read this!!

OKAY NOW, I must admit the idea to have different epilogues for each edition was smart of her 😭 I read I think all of em, and if you wanna know, read below, if you don't, then DO NOT READ PAST THIS!!!

So the standard ending was Lala being shown the mirror of second chances, her deciding not to, but her temptations get the best of her, so she touches it and it stops there.

The alternate ending was Lala going to the mirror of infinite endings instead and doing the same thing there instead of at the mirror of second chances.

The last one was her deciding on not going through any of them, but instead is given a coin and is asked if she ever wishes to choose, just flip the coin and it will tell you which mirror to go through.

I personally liked the first one the most!

ㅤ ‍‍‍‍‍‍ ‍‍‍‍‍‍ ⋅ ☾ This was definitely a book for those of us who came to love these characters and their story on such a deep level. It was the perfect continuation of a love story I had already grown so attached to. And if ACFTL left you wondering whether that was really it in terms of adventures for these two, this book makes it very clear that being happily in love was definitely NOT the end of their story. Their lives are still going to be filled with so many intriguing and fascinating new experiences, and, most importantly, they will FINALLY get to experience them together.

ㅤ ‍‍‍‍‍‍ ‍‍‍‍‍‍ ⋅ ☾ The illustrations are absolutely WONDERFUL. Lotusbubble is such an incredible artist; I already loved her work way before this novella came out, but she did wonders for this book.

ㅤ ‍‍‍‍‍‍ ‍‍‍‍‍‍ ⋅ ☾ I loved the romance, I loved the story, and I loved the premise of it all, BUT I really wish it had been executed better. I wanted it to be MORE. At times, the writing felt a little strange and pulled me out of the atmosphere, and I also felt like some of the plot elements stopped feeling entirely cohesive as the story progressed. Once again, I had the same feeling I had with ACFTL: that there should have been MORE to the story, more development, more time to let everything settle, because once again, it somehow felt unfinished.

ㅤ ‍‍‍‍‍‍ ‍‍‍‍‍‍ ⋅ ☾ Another thing I had a slight issue with was that I feel there were some inconsistencies with the universe Stephanie created in this book.
ㅤ ‍‍‍‍‍‍ ‍‍‍‍‍‍ I don't want to nitpick, and I genuinely didn't mind these things that much, as you can probably tell from my final rating, but it was still a bit... strange... to see these kinds of plot holes.

ㅤ ‍‍‍‍‍‍ ‍‍‍‍‍‍ ⋅ ☾ I DID LOVE the mirror plot (even if it felt a bit silly at one point) ; the legendary lovers energy was going HARD in this book. However, I did feel like the book lacked a bit of just Evangeline and Jacks being together. Everything felt a little rushed, and even after finishing this novella, I was still left craving more of them.
ㅤ ‍‍‍‍‍‍ ‍‍‍‍‍‍ Maybe, just like ACFTL, this book should have been slightly longer. . .just enough to slow things down and actually let us enjoy them a little more. Throughout the book, I just kept wishing we could see a little more of their inner thoughts and feelings. I wanted to understand more deeply what was going through their minds, especially during some of the more emotional moments.

ㅤ ‍‍‍‍‍‍ ‍‍‍‍‍‍ ⋅ ☾ This being said, Jacks and Evangeline are THE legendary lovers and I will die on that hill. If you disagree, you are simply wrong.

ㅤ ‍‍‍‍‍‍ ‍‍‍‍‍‍ ⋅ ♡ Romance... oh my god. I was already hopelessly in love with these two, and I think almost everyone around here knows that by now, but this book still managed to make me swoon, giggle, scream, and feel genuinely happy for them all over again.
ㅤ ‍‍‍‍‍‍ ‍‍‍‍‍‍ It wasn't just them being stupidly and adorably in love. It was all the small gestures, the subtle things they notice about each other, the way they react to one another, and how they constantly put each other before anyone else. The way she never fears anything as long as he is around, the little moments where she simply wants to admire him, and how he immediately notices when something is wrong with her or her demeanour changes. The protection, the constant attention, the absolute adoration, the mutual desire to just be with each other...EVERYTHING. Evangeline will simply always run to Jacks, and Jacks will always look for her. No one else will ever come close to these two and the pure, ridiculous love they have for each other.

ㅤ ‍‍‍‍‍‍ ‍‍‍‍‍‍ ⋅ ☾ I already mentioned above that my main issue with this book, just like with ACFTL, was that it felt too rushed, especially towards the ending. And honestly, the ending itself still didn't leave me feeling fully satisfied. I am genuinely starting to think Stephanie simply doesn't know how to end her books and keeps giving us some vague SOMETHING and calling it a day.
