Zodiac Academy #10

On the Cursed Day of Christmas

Caroline Peckham, Susanne Valenti

3.94

Rate this book
It’s time to deck the halls and return to Solaria for more Christmas chaos than you can shake a buttery bagel at. From the bestselling BookTok sensation romantasy series by the Twisted Sisters, comes a brand-new, fully illustrated hardback advent calendar book experience for Zodiac Academy and Dark Fae fans.

Read a never-before-seen, full-length story inside this fun, festive special edition and open a chapter a day on the run up to Christmas. Inside, readers will discover a brand new tale featuring all their favourite characters! The chapters will be stuffed with gorgeous character and scene art, plus Faebook and Booktok posts that will be #dirtyanddiabolical. Get ready to enjoy the holidays like never before, immersed in the land of Solaria where you can spend Christmas at the Palace of Souls...

T’was the cursed day of Christmas and all through the palace, spirits were lingering with thoughts full of malice.

Did you think Solaria was out of peril at last? Well, the stars aren’t done with their games yet. And you know how they love to ruin the holidays.

On the Cursed Day of Christmas my true love gave to me…

A box of hexed ornaments to decorate my tree.

Yeah… not what I’d been hoping to find on Christmas Eve, but it’s no problem. So long as the enchanted decorations aren’t broken, the malignant entities trapped inside them by a Vega monarch hundreds of years ago can't escape, and the whole of Solaria isn’t threatened by their wrath.

It’s going to be a very merry Christmas indeed.

This festive return to Solaria includes:
24 chapters, sealed behind perforated pages. Open one a day to discover a brand-new Zodiac Academy adventure as you count down to Christmas.
Following on from the events of Restless Stars, fans will love revisiting their favourite characters.
A beautifully packaged hardback, featuring fully illustrated endpapers, character art, stunning scene art and spot illustrations throughout.
The perfect gift for fans of Solaria, and a wonderful alternative to traditional advent calendars.

On the Cursed Day of Christmas was everything I love about the Zodiac Academy world: chaos, humor, heart, and characters that feel like family. I missed this world so much and I am so sad that I am leaving it again. Reading this on Halloween also felt very appropriate given the plot , and yes, can you believe, that for a short novella we actually had a plot.

What made this story so enjoyable was getting to spend more time with the characters after everything they've endured throughout the series. The holiday setting gave them a chance to shine in ways that were both hilarious and heartfelt.

Honestly, this book was pure Christmas chaos and I was here for all of it.The plot was fun, but the characters were what made this such a great read. Seth had me laughing out loud multiple times.His chaotic energy, ridiculous antics, and ability to create trouble without even trying had me laughing throughout the book. Whenever Seth appeared, I knew something entertaining was about to happen. His interactions with Caleb are as funny and lovable as ever, and Caleb's patience with Seth makes their dynamic even better.He's basically a walking catastrophe, and his energy is unmatched. Caleb deserves a medal for dealing with him.

And the texts and Faebook posts are something I will never ever get tired of. They are just hilarious.
Darcy Vega remains one of my favorite characters. Her strength, compassion, and loyalty come through in every challenge she faces, and I loved seeing her balance responsibility with moments of fun and tenderness.

Tory Vega is as fierce and stubborn as ever, bringing plenty of attitude and determination to the story. The bond between the sisters continues to be one of the strongest aspects of the series.

Darius Acrux and Lance Orion were standout characters for me as well. Darius's protective nature and unwavering devotion make him impossible not to love, while Orion's dry humor and willingness to dive headfirst into the madness created some of the book's best moments. Their relationships with Tory and Darcy feel earned and genuine after everything they have been through.

Geraldine remains an icon. A legend. A national treasure. Every time she appears on the page, she steals the scene. Nobody delivers dramatic nonsense quite like Geraldine Grus, and I mean that as the highest compliment.

And I absolutely loved getting more time with Elise and her family. One of my favourite things about the Ruthless Boys crew is how chaotic they are together while still being incredibly devoted to each other. Elise, Leon, Dante, Ryder, and Gabriel bring such a different energy to the story, and every scene with them felt like catching up with old friends. Leon is as outrageous and hilarious as ever, while Dante and Ryder balance out the madness with their protective nature. Seeing them work together while dealing with yet another magical disaster reminded me why they're one of my favourite found families in fantasy. No matter how ridiculous the situation gets, the love and loyalty between them always shines through, and their scenes added so much heart and humour to the story.

This story reminded me why I fell in love with the Zodiac Academy characters in the first place. They're ridiculous, lovable, chaotic, and somehow feel like family by the end. If you're a Zodiac Academy fan, reading this feels like hanging out with old friends, except your friends are all magical disasters. 😂🎄✨

THANK YOU TO MY QUEEN Fariha FOR READING THIS WITH ME
