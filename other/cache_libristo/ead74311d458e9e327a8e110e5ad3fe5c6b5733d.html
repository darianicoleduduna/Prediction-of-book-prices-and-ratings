Overall

The storyline doesn't revolve around sex and is pretty original, so don't let my review dissuade you from reading it, besties xx

Sooo I’ve been on the hunt for a really good mafia romance and… this was not it. I blame BookTok because I searched for dark mafia romances and this kept getting recommended. I’m sorry to anyone who loves this, but it just felt ridiculous to me.

The premise had potential, but the execution completely lost me. Sasha joins the military disguised as a man to avenge her family — except her “disguise” consists of cutting her hair, wrapping a bandage around her chest, and lowering her voice… with zero combat training. And somehow no one notices? Not in the military. Not in the mafia. No one. Only the MMC figures it out for the entire book. I truly had to suspend all disbelief.

And honestly, I could’ve maybe looked past that if Sasha hadn’t been so unbelievably incompetent. She’s supposed to be a bodyguard for an important mafia family, yet she ends up being one of the most frustrating FMCs I’ve read in a long time. The decisions she makes constantly undercut the role we’re told she’s qualified for, and it made it hard to take anything seriously.

I don’t mind dark, toxic dynamics (I read Rina Kent — I know what I signed up for). But I still need emotional vulnerability or growth to balance it out, and I just didn’t feel that payoff here. Neither character felt particularly redeeming. The MMC fully leans into the “monster” persona, which I normally love, but without depth or meaningful development, it just felt unappealing.

The romance itself was strange. I kept waiting for real chemistry to kick in — for that tension, that pull, that spark — and it just never landed. It felt off and difficult to get invested in.

That said, I was entertained just enough to keep turning the pages. Rina Kent’s writing is still bingeable, and there’s a certain chaotic energy that keeps you reading even when you’re rolling your eyes. But once I finished, I didn’t feel compelled to continue the series. I just wish there had been more calculated power plays — those strategic, chess-match moments and simmering tension that make mafia romances feel sharp and addictive.
