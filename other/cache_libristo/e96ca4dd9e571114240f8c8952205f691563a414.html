With this volume, this modern danmei comes to an end. It took me through so many emotions that I still struggle to describe the experience of reading it. More than anything, it felt unique, a story that stayed with me and kept finding new ways to move me from one feeling to another.

This volume doesn’t hold back. It begins with heartbreak and angst, yet love is present throughout, so constant and powerful that at times it nearly blinded me to everything else. As the story unfolds, healing and peace gradually emerge. Jiang Cheng and Gu Fei overcome the obstacles between them and, step by step, build a path toward each other's hearts again. Their new bond grows steadily, until nothing could ever break it again.

Jiang Cheng is a character who has become very dear to me. He is deeply relatable, and every time he achieves something he has worked toward, I can’t help feeling proud of him. He can be naive, but not in a way that makes him seem helpless or unaware of his own strength. Rather, when faced with a problem, he believes he can find a way through it because he trusts himself to keep trying. That confidence is one of his greatest strengths and one of the qualities that helps him move forward in life. Without a doubt, he is one of the strongest characters I’ve had the pleasure of getting to know, and following him as he pursues his dreams has been a joy.

Gu Fei is a character I sometimes struggled to connect with, but even when my affection for Jiang Cheng made me biased, I could never bring myself to dislike him. He had fallen so deeply into darkness that the strength it took to pull himself out of it is especially moving. His progress is not easy or immediate; it comes through the courage to take that first step toward a better future for himself, rather than living only for someone else. At last, he lets go of the shackles that had kept him down and reaches for the person who brought light into his life and doesn't let go again.

Their relationship in this volume shows what true love can look like. It isn’t grand or loud, and it doesn’t need constant declarations to prove itself. Instead, it is a steady presence woven through the story, one that gives both the characters and the readers a sense of security, the feeling that, whatever happens, things will be okay. They both make mistakes that hurt the other, but the author portrays their thought processes and struggles with mental health so thoughtfully that I can understand both of their perspectives. I don’t see either of them as truly at fault; life simply gets in the way, and they have to find their way through it. When they come back together, they do it as they always have: with their emotions laid bare and so much love pouring from one heart to the other.

The author handles mental health with genuine care and sensitivity, while never shying away from its most painful and difficult aspects. The story recognizes how exhausting it can be to keep fighting day after day, even when that fight is mostly invisible to the people around you. People living with mental health struggles show immense strength in continuing to face each day, searching for one more moment of happiness and holding on to the people who love them. I say this as someone who understands how hard and draining it can be to fight with your own mind every single day.

In conclusion, I’m going to miss this danmei, because it is so much more than a love story. It is a story about life, the struggles woven into everyday living, and the hope that someday things will become easier, that one day, we’ll be able to breathe without feeling sad.

This was a beautiful tale, a coming-of-age story that has been brought to a wonderful conclusion. 🥹

Modern danmei can be hard to get into, especially when it comes to finding stories that truly leave a lasting impression. I find this especially true of stories exploring youth and those fleeting, formative years at the threshold between adolescence and adulthood. One thing I often feel is missing from Western literature, and something this story pulls off so genuinely, is the portrayal of authentic, fully realized characters. They aren't shoved into predefined boxes, reduced to familiar archetypes, or shaped by traditional expectations, they're just allowed to exist as individuals, with their own distinct personalities, contradictions, flaws, and complexities. They feel like real people rather than characters designed to fulfil a certain role, and that, to me, is one of the story's greatest strengths.

Maybe it's because I've reached a point where I don't like putting labels on people or things, but that realism felt so refreshing. So much of what they went through hit close to home on a personal level, which made their journey mean that much more to me. I truly just wanted the best for them. ❤️

Across all six volumes, they never feel static or boxed in. They grow, change, make mistakes, and slowly find their own way forward, holding a depth of humanity that feels rare for their age. I'm grateful to have been able to witness their growth.

Heads up: spoilers ahead

Jiang Cheng is our overachiever, the one who pushes through, works hard, and gets things done. For all that determination, and that stubborn drive to keep moving so his tightly wound nerves don't fall apart, he is also such a kind and pure-hearted person. I wouldn't call him naive; there's a difference between naivety and purity, and I think Jiang Cheng embodies the latter. Even when his persistence and stubborn heart are what end up jolting Gu Fei awake, it comes from a place of deep loyalty and care rather than blindness. 🥹

Even with the dark past thrust upon him, being abandoned and drifting without anywhere to truly belong, he never let go of that goodness inside him. He stayed true to himself when everything in his life gave him every reason to harden. Then Gu Fei came along, and his world suddenly opened up, bringing a breath of freedom after years of uncertainty and loneliness. With Gu Fei, he finally found home, sometimes in big ways, and sometimes just sharing a roasted sweet potato in the cold wind and having it taste unexpectedly sweet.

I also love that Jiang Cheng never loses his quirks. Even when he's sick, his instinct to hustle, scheme, and make a little money and stuff like that HAHA. His lucky charm antics are a funny reminder that he's still very much Jiang Cheng, and those lighter moments meant just as much to me as the heavier ones.

Gu Fei is the character I have the most respect for. When all the adults in his life failed him, he was the one left to pick up the pieces. Everything got dumped on him, and somewhere along the way, taking care of everyone else just became automatic, like his own needs didn't even enter the picture.

He often comes across as adult and mature, yet one of the things this story gets right is that growing up also means recognizing your own limits and reinforcing boundaries. Watching him talk things through with Xu Xingzhi and gradually adjust how he cares for Gu Miao shows him finally realizing he can't, and shouldn't, be responsible for everything. Stepping back, stopping himself from accommodating every whim, and shaking off invisible guilt that hooked onto him when it was never his to carry is its own kind of growth. It takes a great deal of courage, emotional maturity, and willingness to change, especially when your circumstances have taught you that letting go is anything but easy.

I love how deeply he loves his sister. The care, tenderness, and devotion he brings to their relationship say so much about his heart. At the same time, I appreciated seeing him slowly understand that loving someone doesn't require sacrificing every part of yourself or fading out in the process, or that his own needs should always come second. Watching him unlearn that was deeply heartwarming, almost like a rainstorm passing and leaving clear skies with room for lightness, joy, and balance again.

And perhaps one of the loveliest things about Gu Fei in this volume is getting to see him loosen up a little. Once that old guilt and heaviness start lifting, he gets to be warmer, lighter, and a bit more playful. Even something as small as adopting a female cat and naming her Cheng-ge made me smile. It's such a silly, affectionate little detail, and it says a lot about how much space Jiang Cheng has come to occupy in his life. Gu Fei finally gets to enjoy being in love, rather than constantly worrying about what that love might cost him. 🐈‍⬛💗

As for Gu Miao, the novel touches on both her autism (which I believe was mentioned at one point, though correct me if I'm remembering incorrectly) and the trauma she's experienced. I'm certainly no expert, and I can only speak from my own experience as an autistic person, but I found her portrayal quite thoughtful. Autism is a spectrum, after all, and I appreciated that her behaviours weren't treated as one-dimensional or necessarily attributed to a single explanation. Her limited verbal communication, difficulty coping with change, need for stability, and reliance on a small circle of people she feels safe with were all aspects I found interesting in how they were portrayed.

I also think it's important to recognize that some of her behaviours may stem from trauma rather than autism alone. The two can intersect and influence one another, and I appreciated that the novel left room for that complexity instead of reducing her character to a single explanation.

I really liked how Gu Fei came to recognize her growth and understand that her world had gradually expanded, even if only a little. And perhaps that was enough. She may never experience independence in quite the way other people do, but that doesn't mean her world cannot continue to grow or that she cannot have a future of her own. What I found touching was the sense that Gu Fei was beginning to understand this, too: that he could have a future of his own while still being there to offer her a helping hand whenever she needed one. That their lives didn't have to be mutually exclusive, and that caring for her didn't mean putting his own life on hold.

Their relationship is truly put to the test here, because a bond isn't proven solely through the fun, easy moments. Holding on through periods of despair, vulnerability, and genuine need is what makes it stronger, and for them, those are the moments where their "I love yous" go beyond any single reason for how they feel, pointing to an unmeasurable bond that nothing else can touch. ❤️

I'm so happy they finally found their way back to each other. In a way, I think the breakup needed to happen. Seeing just how deeply it hurt them made their eventual reunion pack a harder emotional punch, hitting with a sudden, rock-blast kind of impact. And ahh, man, there was Gu Fei, coming to the realization that love was the one thing he couldn't simply bury, suppress, and push his way through. He wanted it, claiming it for himself because feelings aren't something you can just outlast eventually they rise high enough to spill over whatever wall you built. And then there was Jiang Cheng, having to withstand that devastating distance while still wanting Gu Fei to find his own way back to him, to take those steps of his own accord. They couldn't return to where they were before when they were missing each other's traces and catching flashback triggers from the places they knew. They had to confront what they wanted, what they were afraid of losing, and what it meant to choose each other. 🥺

Their love is portrayed beautifully. They could both go on living their lives separately, and the world would keep turning, but there would always be that unmistakable space in each other's lives that only the other could fill. Their lives simply feel so much fuller together. 💗

And I think volume 6 does a lovely job of showing us what that fullness actually looks like: the stolen hand squeezes and exchanged glances when they know what they feel for each other, yet have to hold back because of their complicated separation; alongside moments of leaning half-asleep against Gu Fei's shoulder in the car while holding hands, and all those little expressions of affection. It's also there in the more playful moments: the teasing, the shared laughter, and the freedom to be a little ridiculous with someone who knows you so well. They just match each other, carrying and baring it all together.

One of my favorite lines is the one from the old notebook: "May we both be as brave as each other." Neither of them has had an easy run in life, and both had to find their own ways to get through survival mode. Somewhere along the way, though, they became a source of courage for each other. They helped each other realize that survival mode didn't have to be permanent and that being brave doesn't always mean toughing it out by yourself

And then Gu Fei finally says "I love you" out loud in the sunlight while they're on horseback, leaving Jiang Cheng nearly sliding off the horse in a massive static shock. I couldn't help smiling. Saying it so openly felt straight from the heart, no jokes, no holding back, just finally saying it out loud LMAOOO. 😭❤️

I did skim through the extras a little though XD Some of the slice-of-life ones felt repetitive to the point I just got bored with it, though the time skips were very nice to see where everyone else and their relationship ended up!!

A beautiful ending to a story and a journey I'm going to remember very fondly. If I had to sum it up, this story has heart and it feels alive. So out there, cheers to Jiang Cheng and Gu Fei! May they have a whole string of blessings, happiness, and..... well, whatever adversities life throws their way, because I know they can handle it with courage as long as they have each other. 💖

Con una tristeza grandísima tengo que decir que voy a hacer esta review. La tristeza únicamente se debe a que ya he terminado todos los libros de esta saga tan sumamente preciosa y llena de tanta emoción que, omg, omg, omg, me voy a llorar una vez más.

Y yo, cada vez que leía estas cosas, no podía más que sufrir por él, porque sé que todo esto es su forma de protegerse, porque de verdad que no quería hacerle daño a Jiang Cheng y, a lo mejor, de esta forma podía seguir con los ojos cerrados durante más tiempo.

Y luego, por el otro lado, teníamos a Jiang Cheng, que aunque estuviese dolido, como que una parte de él ni siquiera era consciente de que de verdad lo habían dejado, pero estaba dispuesto a hacer lo necesario por Gu Miao y seguir pidiendo ayuda a Xu Xingzhi. Y omg, el primer encuentro se me hizo tan doloroso, me dolía verlos tener que comportarse de esa forma un poco más fría a pesar de que lo único que querían era besarse y estar juntos para siempre. Una cosa que me gustó mucho fue Xu Xingzhi hablando con Jiang Cheng y diciéndole que muchos de los miedos y reacciones que tiene Gu Miao son debido a Gu Fei; como ya mencioné anteriormente, es una persona con muchos traumas que nunca ha ido a un psicólogo, por lo tanto no se estaba dando cuenta de todos los problemas mentales que estaba teniendo y que encima se los estaba inculcando a su hermana.

Pero esta vez no está siendo el caso y, como bien estaba diciendo Jiang Cheng, iba a esperar hasta que Gu Fei decidiese abrir los ojos, y ahí estaría él, esperándole, porque nunca se rendiría con Gu Fei. Y, dios mío, me pareció tan bonito que solo podía seguir llorando. Me parece que en este tomo ambos aprenden mucho de cómo gestionar las cosas, y obviamente Xu Xingzhi ha tenido mucho que ver; los dos necesitaban a un psicólogo que les ayudara a avanzar y ver que hay una salida a esos momentos tan oscuros.

Y dios mío, cuando estaba viendo que Gu Miao estaba avanzando y hacía cosas fuera de su burbuja y que todo estaba saliendo bien, me dio un vuelvo en el corazón y solo podía llorar de lo feliz que estaba por ella y por las personas que la rodean. Me alegro tanto de que hayan podido encontrar a alguien que les diera esa esperanza y ese tratamiento para que mi niña pudiera entenderse también a ella misma, y ser más feliz haciendo cosas y sobre todo disfrutar de la vida, porque al final es una niña súper buena y súper empática. Y dios mío, qué preciosidad lo feliz que se ponía con los animales o al hacer actividades que le gustaban y, buah, no puedo, me pongo tan feliz.

Y luego otra vez me puse a llorar cuando van todos de viaje, y le dice Gu Fei a Jiang Cheng que gracias por no rendirse con él, que gracias a él está viviendo algo que nunca se pensaba que iba a pasar, y me muero, porque por fin Gu Fei se estaba dando la oportunidad de vivir, de no ser únicamente esa persona responsable de todos los problemas de la casa y de hacer todo, sino que estaba VIVIENDO, encima con su hermana y con la persona de la cual está profundamente enamorada. Fue super bonito leer todo eso, me emocionaba tanto ver todo el cambio que ha conseguido hacer; estábamos viendo a un Gu Fei tan distinto del primer libro que, uff, lágrimas. Era más abierto con lo que sentía y con lo que quería, y no puedo pedir nada más para él, simplemente que sea feliz para siempre con la gente a la que quiere.

Sé que Jiang Cheng siempre está diciendo que no es detallista o que no es un romántico, pero al fin y al cabo él tiene una forma distinta de demostrar las cosas que Gu Fei, y aun así lo hacía de forma súper bonita; idk, las cosas que le decía a Gu Fei, las acciones... decían mucho de lo enamorado que estaba y era súper bonito.

Así que sí, no podría haber pedido un final más bonito para esta saga tan preciosa, que lo único que hace es acariciarte constantemente el alma, porque todo está escrito con un cariño y un amor que no tiene sentido. Es una historia que te llena de emoción y tristeza por la complejidad de los personajes y los problemas que tienen, pero que al final consiguen superar todo eso y tener una vida feliz con gente que los quiere mucho.

Pan Zhi siempre será mi niño lindo y el mejor amigo que uno puede tener; también me gustó mucho el cambio que tuvo con las relaciones y sobre todo cuando vio que el vínculo que tienen estos dos merece la pena y que Jiang Cheng tenía que luchar por ello.

Así que sí, con el corazón en la mano diré que es de mis sagas favoritas del mundo mundial, no tiene sentido todo lo que me ha hecho sentir y lo feliz que estoy de haber leído algo tan sumamente bello.

Gu Fei & Jiang Cheng! Final volume :(

I’m so glad we got this final volume to resolve the issues from vol 5 and, more importantly, give us some happy moments with Gu Fei and Jiang Cheng. After everything these two have been through, they deserved it!

I absolutely loved Gu Fei’s character development in this volume. Seeing him grow into someone who is willing to invest in his own future was so satisfying. He’s spent so much of his life putting everyone else first, and it was wonderful to see him finally start to believe that he deserves something for himself, too.

Jiang Cheng also matures, while still being the same silly teenage boy we know and love. I really appreciated that balance. He’s growing up, but he hasn’t lost that youthful energy and ridiculousness that make him so endearing.

The extras were fun, too! I was convinced they were giving us more of an HFN than a definitive HEA, but honestly, I do believe these two care enough about each other to go the distance. Their relationship has never been easy, and I think that’s what makes me root for them so much.

Also, the adults in this story are SO hands-off. It’s just wild to me. And Gu Miao? The ultimate cool girl. No notes.

Overall, for the series: 4/5 stars.

While this was a cute and emotional story, there were a couple of storylines that felt slower to me, particularly the studying and basketball tournament arcs. I also wish some of the side characters had been more fleshed out. It was nice to see them reappear in the extras, though!

This was my first modern danmei, and I think it was a really good introduction to the genre. I got invested in Gu Fei and Jiang Cheng making their relationship work despite their very different circumstances. Their story is such a good representation of the challenges of young love, especially when life keeps throwing obstacles in your way.

I’m glad we got to see them grow, struggle, and find their way back to each other. And while I’m sad to say goodbye to these two, I’m happy with where their story leaves them. ❤️
