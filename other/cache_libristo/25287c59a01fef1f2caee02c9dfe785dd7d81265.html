Serena Paris est orpheline, sans meute, et unique en son genre. En révélant son statut de première hybride humaine-louve-garou, elle était censée remédier à une division vieille de plusieurs siècles entre les espèces. À la place, elle devient une cible, la proie d'impitoyables machinations politiques entre loups-garous, vampyres et humains. Acculée par ses ennemis, elle n'a plus qu'une seule option... s'il veut bien d'elle.

En tant qu'alpha de la meute du Nord-Ouest, Koen Alexander force l'obéissance. Son autorité est si absolue que seul un fou oserait menacer sa compagne. Qu'importe que Serena ne partage pas ses sentiments : rien ne l'empêchera de la protéger.

Mais les vampyres et loups-garous avides de pouvoir ne sont pas les seules menaces qui pèsent sur Serena. Tôt ou tard, son passé finira par la rattraper, et Koen pourrait bien être le seul à pouvoir la sauver de l'anéantissement...

"He told her that he would never touch her because he didn’t want her enough; in truth, he will never touch her because he wants her too much. "

After surviving the harrowing kidnapping from book 1, Serena is back (if a bit traumatized) with a whole new problem on her hands.

Turns out she's half werewolf, and it feels like the whole world is waiting for a chance to snuff her out. On top of that, she feels like absolute shit, can't shift, and did she mention feeling like death warmed over?

When Serena gets her diagnosis, she decides that she needs to be apart for a bit - and that works, almost. Everyone gives her space, except for her mouthy Alpha Koen, who just so happens to be her mate...and who has publicly declared that he absolutely refuses to be with her.

Great. Just great. Well, hopefully she will find a way to get over him as well...cause the more time she spends with him, the more she wants to be by his side for every moment of the day.

UGH.

The whole time I was reading this one, I kept thinking: book 2 better be half as good at book 1!! Cause hotdamn, I finished the first book in a single day.

Careful what you wish for...cause this one was half as good...but that means we went from 4 stars to 2.

Ultimately what threw me out of the book was the meanness, the circular plot, and the overly gratuitous sex.

The Meanness

“She’s an adult Were who doesn’t require coddling,” Koen interrupts. Once again, a bit jarring to hear, especially as he rolls thick, soft socks up my shins.

Look, I get Koen is supposed to be the gruff-exterior-fluffy-inside kind of character but I really grew tired of the shtick.

"I’m simply going to lock you up, killer. If I have to chain you to my fucking bed to keep you alive, I will not hesitate."

His insults were supposed to be funny, but they just came off as mean to me - maybe it was the aggressive way that the audiobook narrator threw each barbed comment at the audience.

The whole time that Serena was falling for him, I kept feeling disgusted by his behavior and verbal "quips".

He sighs. tightens his hold on me. "Such a fucking nuisance".

Seriously, if someone I loved said even half the stuff he threw at her or treated me the way she was treated, I would seriously consider cutting them from my life.

And I get we're supposed to use the whole 'he was traumatized' and 'it's for the better good' excuse...but seriously. He was being a fuckwaffle to her. And I'm tired of being told that guys who treat girls like that are supposed to be the love interest.

The Circular Plot

Aka, it took freaking forever to get anywhere. I don't want to give away too much, but basically we have a bit of plot in the beginning, lots of spiraling, worrying, and more spiraling, then a smidge of plot at the end.

Oh and like 5 hours of sex scenes.

The Overly Gratuitous Sex

It was just... a lot.

A lot of pining over whether sex would happen, a lot of buildup to the scenes and a freaking lot of scenes.

I grew bored with it. I much preferred the first book which had a lot of spice, but that was after a lot of plot and mystery happened.

This book, I figured out the cause of the mystery right away (well, anyone who's read werewolf fanfic would know this answer) and then had to waste a lot of time waiting for the rest of the characters to catch up...and then decide what to do about it (sex, the answer was always sex).

Overall Thoughts

I really enjoyed this world the first time around, the second time was such a meh time that I am starting to question why I liked book 1.

He’s protective over her in that quietly intense way that has me feral. No one else is allowed to fuss over her. Just him 😭
